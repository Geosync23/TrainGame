Railhead station rework

You asked for: no more running back to the ticket counter, more trains coming and going, every train seen coming in to the platform, closer switches, no Overland, checking tickets on the train before it leaves, a separate cargo track and area where freight trains are unloaded, and trains with more cars that are still small and cute. Up to 5 helpers. A few choices below decide how the helpers build it.

The new flow at the hub

  • A passenger train comes in from the end of the line, visibly. You throw the switch so it runs into the platform. Travellers step off and new ones board.
  • Before it leaves you walk along the train and check everyone's ticket. Then you set the switches for its way out and send it off.
  • A freight train comes in to its own cargo track beside a cargo yard and shed. Unloading takes a while, and passenger trains keep coming while it stands there. When it is empty you set the switches and see it off.
  • The hub's switches sit close together near the platform and cargo track, so throwing them is a few steps, not a long walk.
  • The Overland goes (parked, not deleted). Goods you order come on the freight trains instead.
  • Trains get longer: a passenger train of 3 or 4 short cars, a freight train of 4 or 5. They stay small and cute.
