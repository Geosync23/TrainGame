Railhead clarity fixes

You said it isn't clear where to take a contract, which train is yours, or why the hub board says "No engine on the timetable". You're right: the game hides all three. Here is why, and what I suggest.

Why it's confusing now

  • Contracts are only taken at the telegraph key in the depot window or at the office desk. Nothing in the world points to either, and the only hint is a telegram.
  • Your train is engine No. 1, but it looks like every other engine. The Overland's trains, which aren't yours, also pass through the hub.
  • "No engine on the timetable" means the two Willow Ford runs exist but no engine is assigned to them. A new game starts that way. You have to tap No. 1 and put it on a run, and the game only says so in the last tutorial telegram.

The tutorial arrows being built now will point at the telegraph key and at No. 1 from the start. The questions below cover the bigger changes. They go in after the four builds now running are merged and tested.
