One question while the helpers build

Which side faces you

With one fixed view, the main platform sits behind its track, so a standing passenger train hides the line of travellers and you while you punch tickets. The same happens at the freight platform: the freight train hides the spot where you unload. The picture on the left is a builder's test (placeholder drawing, not the final look): the green coaches cover the platform behind them.

  • Turn the station round (my suggestion). Same order you chose (station, platform, two tracks, island platform, freight track), but laid out so the platforms are on the side nearest you: you see the travellers, yourself and the freight doors in front of the trains. The station building and shed are then seen from their other side (the artist draws them turned round), and the street runs towards the bottom of the screen. Costs about a day of rework for the layout and two buildings.
  • Keep it as it is and see through. A train or building in front of you turns see-through (a dotted see-through look with outlines) while you or a line are behind it. No rework, but you often play through a see-through train.
