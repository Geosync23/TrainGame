Separate style test, for your decision

Richer pixel style test

Our station drawn in the richer style of the pictures you shared: brighter colours, a sky band, lush trees and flowers, bigger people with readable faces, and a little sparkle. Only the style was borrowed; everything drawn is our own 1866 station. This was made on the side; the game being built is unchanged.

What it would take

  • Everything is drawn twice as big. People go from 14 to about 32 pixels tall. A phone screen then shows less than half as much of the station at once, so you would pan or pinch between a close view and an overview more often.
  • People: almost all redrawn, about 4 to 6 times the work of the current set, mainly the walking and boarding animations at the bigger size.
  • Buildings and trains: the shapes carry over; the surfaces get new detail (planks, shingles, windows, flowers, vines): about 1.5 to 2 times today's work.
  • New: foliage, a sky band pinned to the top of the screen, and sparkle, built from scratch.
  • Overall: roughly 2 to 3 times the art work done so far.
  • A middle path: keep today's size, but take the new colours, foliage, sky and sparkle, with people a little bigger (about 20 pixels). Much of the richness for a fraction of the work. Not mocked up yet.
