Before the overnight build: questions

Building the line, the crew and the bigger town

You asked me to ask before starting. Each question has my suggestion marked; change any you like and add notes. When you are done, say "answered" in the chat and the team of five starts.

What I understood

  • Fixes first: the first freight's goods cannot be lifted out (a bug, I will find it); loading and unloading much easier; a much smaller cart; faster walking; see-through effects blended, not square.
  • Line building, its own mode: a work train leaves early, before the first passenger train, with the crew (5 men to start, up to 30) and supplies. Each man is simulated on his own. They dig the bed, lay the rock, then the beams, the rails and the last stage. You can join in at any stage or watch.
  • A long strip of country like a wide highway, with the line slowly filling it and another town at the far end. A full crew of 30 takes about two game weeks; little track is laid each day.
  • Workers: ticket punchers, cargo loaders, delivery men and rail workers. All come home at night and are counted like the townsfolk. A tent camp with a bed for each rail worker that grows with the crew.
  • A bigger town: every building can be entered, houses for everyone, each person has a role, and the trains change the town's fortunes.
  • Dev mode: hiring, crew size and "be a rail worker" are buttons there. The game still starts as it does now. Progression comes later.
  • A longer day.

Things I will decide myself unless you say otherwise in the last note: a smaller crew is slower in proportion (5 men take about six times as long as 30); the supply train is always full for now; the far town is drawn but not yet playable.
