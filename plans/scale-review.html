What changed

  • Bigger world, same people. Track, trains and cars, the platform, buildings and distances are 1.6 times bigger. The engine and tender are now about 8 people long. The map is 640 by 320 units, with open land around the hub. Willow Ford is still 10 miles away.
  • Same times. Trains run faster to match, so the timetable, fares and wood use are as before. You walk a quarter faster. Hand-building steps are quicker, so a siding of the same shape takes the same real time.
  • Camera. It starts a little closer (1.5x), and you can zoom out to 0.4x to see a whole train and platform.
  • Feet on the floor. You stand on the platforms and the freight dock and step up and down at their edges.
  • Three small gaps fixed. Materials put down at a building job can be picked back up. A switch inside a platform track no longer cancels its trains. An item in the wrong bay shows a yellow warning mark.

Choices made while building

  • The platform and dock stay at a single step's height. At 1.6 times they would come above a person's knee.
  • Things a person handles stay person-sized: switch stands, benches, carried goods, the hand truck and the handcart.
  • The spots where you stand beside a car stay within arm's reach of the wider cars.
  • The bigger train blocked the way to the water tower spout, so fuelling at the hub failed. That is fixed: you can now walk the last short bit straight to a spot beside a train.
