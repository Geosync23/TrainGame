What changed in this round

  • A bigger world. Track, trains, buildings and distances are 1.6 times bigger around the people; the map is 640 by 320 units. You approved this size.
  • Wide plank platforms. Both platforms are 9.6 units deep, made of weathered planks, with steps at the ends.
  • The platform roof turns see-through when you are under it, near it, or behind it from the camera.
  • Feet on the floor. You stand on platforms, the dock and the booth floor.
  • A calmer clock. One game minute lasts 1 real second; a game day is 24 real minutes. The timetable still fits.
  • A new walk for you and everyone else: feet stay planted, arms swing, the body bobs and leans.
  • Crossings. Track is walked across only on plank crossings: at both ends of each platform, west of the freight shed, and at Willow Ford. You can build your own with the Build tool (tap a track; one tie bundle). Switches, couplings, wood and water work from either side.
  • The cutaway. When you board, the combine opens: you in the baggage end, travellers seated, freight by the wall.
  • Departure boards on both depot walls, showing the next 4 departures; tap one for the card.
  • The ticket booth. You sell tickets from inside the depot, behind the window; the depot turns see-through while you are in it.
  • Station detail. Trim, a stovepipe and name boards on the depots; a notice board, baggage wagon, freight scale, crates, barrels, milk cans, planters and a hitching rail.
  • Three small gaps fixed. Materials at a building job can be picked back up; a switch beside a platform no longer cancels trains; items in the wrong bay show a warning mark.
